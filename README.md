# Write a Song AI

**Write a song with AI: describe it in one sentence or paste your own lyrics, and get a finished song with melody and vocals in a minute or two.**

🌐 Website: **[writeasongai.com](https://writeasongai.com)** · 中文: [writeasongai.com/zh](https://writeasongai.com/zh)

Write a Song AI is an online AI song writer for people with no music background. Tell it who the song is for or what it is about, pick a style, and it writes the lyrics, composes the melody and sings it. Every generation gives you two versions to play in your browser. Already have lyrics? Paste them and hear them sung as written.

## What it does

- **Write a song from an idea.** One sentence is enough: a birthday, an anniversary, a love song, a lullaby.
- **Turn your lyrics into a song.** Paste up to 5,000 characters, mark sections with `[Verse]` and `[Chorus]`, choose a style and voice. Your words are sung as written. → [Lyrics to song](https://writeasongai.com/lyrics-to-song)
- **Two versions every time,** with a title, lyrics and vocals, or instrumentals only.
- **Styles:** pop, country, rap, rock, R&B, lo-fi, lullaby, love songs and more.
- **Private library.** Only you can see and play your songs.
- **Credits back on failure.** If a generation fails without a usable track, its songs are returned automatically.
- **English and Chinese** interface, prompts and lyrics.

## How it works

1. **Describe your song,** or paste your lyrics.
2. **Pick a style** and a voice, or go instrumental.
3. **Generate and listen** to two versions, then download them on a paid plan.

## Model

Songs are generated with Suno's v6 music models (Suno v6, v6 Mini and v6 Wild) through an API provider. Write a Song AI is an independent product and is not affiliated with Suno.
